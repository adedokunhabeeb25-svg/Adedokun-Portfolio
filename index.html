FEATURED CASE STUDY

Verified Artisan Hub

Helping people quickly find and book trusted local service providers.

MVP
Service marketplace

The problem

People often struggle to quickly find trusted local artisans and understand who is available, verified and suitable for an urgent job.

The approach

I mapped the client and artisan journeys, focused the MVP on discovery, verification, availability, booking, payment and reviews.

Key screens

Job posting · Match list · Artisan profile · Verification · Availability · Checkout · Completion · Rating · Artisan dashboard.

01Post a jobDescribe the task and need.
02Find a matchSee trusted available artisans.
03Book & payConfirm the service securely.
04Complete & rateClose the job and leave feedback.
